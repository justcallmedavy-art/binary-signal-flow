FAQ

Is this affiliated with Deriv?

No. It is an independent front end built on Deriv's public API and OAuth. Deriv trademarks belong to their owners.

Do you hold my funds or my password?

Never. Login happens on Deriv's own OAuth page and trades are placed from your browser straight to Deriv's WebSocket API.

Can I test without risking money?

Yes — switch to your virtual (demo) account in the account menu before pressing Run. All bot runs work identically on demo.

Ready to let the machine watch the candles?

Sign in with Deriv, open Bot Builder and press Run.
